How I did it
  1. Study the brand. What it stands for and what's already working.
  2. Study the niche. The topics people keep sending each other.
  3. Study the audience. What they feel, and what they'd save for later.
  4. Write once, use twice. One script, cut as a reel and designed as a carousel.
